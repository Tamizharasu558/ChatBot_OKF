# Frequently Asked Questions

### What subscription tiers are available?

We provide three pricing tiers:
1. **Starter ($29/month)**: Includes up to 5 team members, standard API access (100 req/min), and email support.
2. **Professional ($99/month)**: Includes up to 25 team members, enhanced API access (500 req/min), and 12-hour response SLA.
3. **Enterprise (Custom pricing)**: Unlimited seats, dedicated account manager, 1,000 req/min rate limit, SSO/SAML integration, and 99.99% uptime SLA.

### What is the refund policy?

We offer a **14-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee** on all new subscription purchases.[^terms-of-service] 
- If you request a cancellation within 14 calendar days of your initial payment, our billing team will issue a 100% refund back to your original payment method.
- Refunds typically appear in your bank account within 3 to 5 business days.
- Annual contracts canceled after the 14-day window are non-refundable, but access continues until the end of the contracted billing period without renewal.

### What payment methods are supported?

We accept:
- Major credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.
- Digital wallets: Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- ACH Direct Debit and Wire Transfers for Enterprise annual contracts over $5,000 USD.

### How do I download tax invoices and receipts?

Account administrators can access all historical invoices by navigating to **Settings > Billing & Invoices** in the customer web portal. Every invoice includes our corporate VAT/tax registration identifier and itemized charges formatted as a downloadable PDF.
